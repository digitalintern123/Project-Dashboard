# Encalm Projects – Video Tutorial Script (draft for review)

**Format:** one video with 12 chapters, about 12 minutes
**Audience:** Project Leads and Project Coordinators
**Voice:** free offline voice, Indian English, female
**Sample data:** realistic sample projects (Delhi T3 International Lounge, Hyderabad Airport Hotel, Goa Central Kitchen and others). No real company data appears.
**On screen:** orange highlight boxes and numbered badges, as in the manual, with English subtitles burned in.

Each chapter opens with a 2-second title card. **[SCREEN]** says what the viewer sees. **VO** is the narration, word for word. *(~0:40)* is the target length.

---

## 0. Opening *(~0:30)*

**[SCREEN]** Title card: "Encalm Projects – How to use the dashboard". Then the sign-in page.

**VO:**
Welcome to Encalm Projects, the dashboard where we plan, track and report every Encalm project, from the first brief to handover.
In this video, Project Leads will learn how to keep their projects up to date, and Project Coordinators will learn how to run the whole portfolio.
Coordinators' sections are marked on screen. Let's begin.

---

## 1. Signing in and setting your password *(~0:50)*

**[SCREEN]** Type the email address. Type the password and click the eye icon. Click **Sign in**. The **Change password** screen appears. Fill in the three boxes and click **Update password and continue**.

**VO:**
Open the app in Chrome or Edge, and sign in with your Encalm email address and the temporary password you were given.
Click the eye icon if you want to check what you typed.
The first time you sign in, the app asks you to choose your own password. Enter the temporary password, then a new one, at least ten characters, with both letters and numbers, and type it again to confirm.
Click "Update password and continue". You stay signed in on this browser for up to seven days.
If you forget your password, ask a Project Coordinator to reset it.

---

## 2. Finding your way around *(~0:45)*

**[SCREEN]** Highlight, in turn: the left menu, the role label, the notification bell, Settings, and Logout.

**VO:**
The menu on the left is the same on every page. From here you can reach the Portfolio overview, All projects, New project, My projects, and portfolio-wide views of the Timeline, Milestones, Issues and risks, Updates, Site photographs and Budget.
At the top you can see the role you are signed in with, your notifications, and Logout.
You never need to refresh the page. The app picks up your colleagues' changes by itself every few seconds.

---

## 3. Reading the dashboard *(~0:50)*

**[SCREEN]** Portfolio overview. Highlight the key figures strip, then Portfolio health, then Management attention. Click one at-risk project, then go back.

**VO:**
The Portfolio overview is your home page.
Across the top are the key figures: active projects, the total approved budget, or AOP, and how much of it is unspent, average progress, and how many projects need attention.
Portfolio health shows how many projects are on track, at risk or delayed.
Management attention lists the projects that are at risk or delayed. Click one to open it.

---

## 4. Finding a project *(~0:40)*

**[SCREEN]** Type "lounge" into the search box. Click **Filters**, choose a location, then clear the filters. Click a row in the register.

**VO:**
To find a project, type part of its name, location, code or lead in the search box.
Click Filters to narrow the list by location, category, status and health.
The portfolio register lists every matching project with its status, health, progress and budget. Click a row to open the project.

---

## 5. Creating a new project *(~1:10)*

**[SCREEN]** Click **New project**. Fill in the name "Mumbai Departure Lounge". Open Location, choose **Other location…** and type "Mumbai". Choose the category, the target date and the AOP. *Coordinator view:* highlight **Allot to Project Lead**. Click **Create project**.

**VO:**
Project Leads and Coordinators can create projects. Click New project in the menu.
Give the project a clear name.
For Location, pick one of the listed airports, or choose "Other location" and type a new one, for example Mumbai. The new location is then available in every filter.
Choose the category, the target completion date and the approved budget in crores. Fields marked with a star are required.
If you are a Coordinator, you also choose which Project Lead the project is allotted to. A project a Lead creates is allotted to them automatically.
Click Create project. Every new project starts with five stages: Brief and scope, Design development, Procurement, Execution and Handover.

---

## 6. Inside a project *(~0:50)*

**[SCREEN]** Delhi T3 International Lounge. Highlight the status and health, Edit project, the key figures, then the tab bar.

**VO:**
This is the project page. At the top are the project's status and health, which the Lead or a Coordinator can change.
Click Edit project to change the name, dates, location, area or budget figures.
Below that are the key figures: completion, approved budget, projected cost and days to the target date.
Everything else lives in the tabs: Overview, Progress, Timeline, Milestones, Site photographs, Commercial, Issues and risks, and Updates.
If you are not allowed to edit a project, you will see "View only" in the header.

---

## 7. Updating a stage *(~1:20)*

**[SCREEN]** **Timeline** tab. Click **Update stage** on Execution. Change Progress from 45 to 60. Fill in Work completed and Next action. Click **Save stage update**. The bar changes colour and percentage. Then click the bell to show the notification as a Coordinator would see it.

**VO:**
Keeping stages up to date is the most important job in the app. Open the Timeline tab and click Update stage on the stage you are working on.
Set the progress percentage. The status follows along: above zero is In progress, and one hundred percent is Complete. If work has stopped, set it to Blocked.
Write what has been done in Work completed, and what happens next in Next action.
If you need a decision from management, write it in Decision required. Everyone is alerted.
Click Save stage update. The bar colour shows the stage's state: green for complete, blue for in progress, red for blocked.
HODs, Coordinators and the project's Lead get a notification every time a stage moves. Reaching Complete or becoming Blocked is highlighted.
If a save ever fails, a red message tells you why, and your change is not kept, so you can try again.

---

## 8. Progress, milestones and approvals *(~1:15)*

**[SCREEN]** **Progress** tab: highlight Total Project Delivery. Then the **Milestones** tab: add "Fire NOC received" with **Requires approval** ticked. *Coordinator view:* click **Approve** on a pending milestone. Then click **Complete** on another milestone.

**VO:**
The Progress tab shows how each stage adds up to the project's total completion. You don't type the total. It is calculated from the stages.
Milestones are the key dates of a project. On the Milestones tab, enter a name, date, stage and owner, and click Add milestone.
If a milestone needs sign-off, tick "Requires approval". Every Coordinator is then asked to approve it.
Coordinators: open the milestone and click Approve or Reject. Only Coordinators can do this.
When a milestone is done, click Complete, and it moves to the completed list.

---

## 9. Issues and risks *(~0:55)*

**[SCREEN]** **Issues & risks** tab. Click **Add issue or risk** and fill it in, choosing severity High. Change the status of an existing issue to **Action in progress**.

**VO:**
Use Issues and risks to record anything that threatens the schedule, cost or quality.
Give each issue a title, a description, an owner, a target closure date, and its impact on time and cost.
Choose the severity carefully. A High issue is treated as critical: HODs and Coordinators are emailed, and the project appears under Needs attention on the dashboard.
As work continues, move the issue along: Open, Under review, Action in progress, Resolved, and Closed.

---

## 10. Updates, tagging and email *(~1:10)*

**[SCREEN]** **Updates** tab. Click **Add stage update** and type "@anit". The suggestion list appears; pick the person. Click **Post update**. Then, on a stage, click **Tag Member**, tick a person, check **Will email**, and click **Tag & Send Mail**.

**VO:**
The Updates tab is the project diary. Click Add stage update, choose the stage and kind, and write what changed.
To bring someone in, type the at sign and the start of their name, then pick them from the list. Small typos are fine.
Click Post update. Tagged people get a notification and an email.
You can also tag people on any stage, milestone or issue with Tag Member. Tick the people, add a message, check the "Will email" list, and click Tag and Send Mail.
Emails are always sent from your own Encalm mailbox, so replies come straight back to you.

---

## 11. Site photographs and budget *(~1:00)*

**[SCREEN]** **Site Photograph** tab. Click **Upload Site Photographs**, drop in 3 photos, set the batch stage and category, and click **Upload**. Then the **Commercial** tab: click **Edit commercial data** and change Spent till date.

**VO:**
Keep a photo record of progress on the Site photograph tab. Click Upload, and select up to twenty photos at once from your phone or computer.
Set the stage, category and date once for the whole batch, add captions if you like, and click Upload. The team is notified.
The Commercial tab tracks money against the approved budget: committed, projected and spent so far. An alert appears if costs go over the AOP.
Please update "Spent till date" regularly, so the dashboard's figures stay accurate.

---

## 12. For Coordinators: team, allotment and executive updates *(~1:20)*

**[SCREEN]** **Team & Allotment** page. Highlight **Create Project Lead** (example name only, not submitted). Highlight **Reset password** on a lead. Change the lead in a project's **Allot to** dropdown. Then, on a project, click **Email Update**, add recipients with "@", type commentary, and highlight **Send Branded Update Email** without sending.

**VO:**
This section is for Project Coordinators.
The Team and Allotment page is where you manage Project Leads. Create a new Lead with their name and email address, and give them their temporary password privately, never in a group chat.
If a Lead forgets their password, click Reset password next to their name.
To move a project to a different Lead, choose them in the Allot to list. The new Lead can edit the project straight away.
To send management a polished summary of a project, open the project and click Email Update. Add recipients with the at sign, write your commentary, and click Send Branded Update Email.

---

## 13. Closing *(~0:25)*

**[SCREEN]** Back to the Portfolio overview. End card: "Need help? Contact your Project Coordinator" and the app address.

**VO:**
That's it. Keep your stages, milestones and issues up to date, and the dashboard will tell everyone where each project stands.
The full user manual has more detail on every screen. If you need help, contact your Project Coordinator.
Thank you for watching.
