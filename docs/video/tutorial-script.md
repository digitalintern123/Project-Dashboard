# Encalm Projects – Video Tutorial Script v4 (for review)

**Format:** one video in two parts, about 16 minutes
**Personas, the same throughout:** **Priya Sharma**, Project Coordinator · **Rahul Mehta**, Project Lead
**Flow:** each part starts with a **role title card** (who they are and what they can do), then follows the work **in order**.
- Priya sets up the team first, then oversees the portfolio.
- Rahul creates **one project, Delhi T2 Departure Lounge**, then goes through its tabs **left to right**: Overview → Progress → Timeline → Milestones → Site Photograph → Commercial → Issues & risks → Updates.

**On screen:**
- a role badge in the top-right corner for each part, e.g. "PROJECT COORDINATOR VIEW · Priya Sharma";
- ★ marks a **pop-out**: the screen dims, the explained part is enlarged, and a short label appears beside it;
- English subtitles throughout.

**Voice pronunciation** (subtitles keep the normal spelling):
- Encalm = "EN-calm";
- AOP, HOD, NOC are read as letters;
- amounts are read as "48 crore rupees".

---

## Opening (~0:30)
**[SCREEN]** Title card, then the sign-in page.

1. Welcome to Encalm Projects, the dashboard where we plan, track and report every Encalm project, from the first brief to handover.
2. This video has two parts: first for Project Coordinators, who run the portfolio, then for Project Leads, who keep their projects up to date.
3. Let's start with the Project Coordinator.

---

# PART 1 – PROJECT COORDINATOR

**[ROLE TITLE CARD, ~0:25]**
> **Part 1 · Project Coordinator**
> **Priya Sharma** · runs the project portfolio
>
> **What a Project Coordinator can do**
> - See and edit every project
> - Add Project Leads and reset their passwords
> - Allot projects to Project Leads
> - Approve or reject milestones
> - Delete projects
> - Send Email Updates to management

**VO:**
1. A Project Coordinator runs the whole portfolio.
2. Coordinators can see and edit every project, add Project Leads, allot projects to them, approve milestones, and send updates to management.

**[Badge for the whole part]** PROJECT COORDINATOR VIEW · Priya Sharma

### Step 1 · Signing in (~0:30)
**[SCREEN]** Priya types her email and password and clicks Sign in.

1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge and sign in with your Encalm email address and password. ★ *Sign in*
2. You stay signed in on that browser for up to seven days.

### Step 2 · Finding your way around (~0:40)
**[SCREEN]** The cursor moves down the menu, then to the top bar.

1. The menu on the left is the same on every page. ★ *Main menu*
2. As a Coordinator, you also see Team and Allotment, which only Coordinators can use. ★ *Team & Allotment*
3. At the top are your role, your notifications and Logout. ★ *Role · Notifications*
4. You never need to refresh the page; the app picks up everyone's changes by itself.

### Step 3 · Adding a Project Lead (~0:45)
**[SCREEN]** Team & Allotment. Type "Rahul Mehta" and his email into Create Project Lead (not submitted); pop out the password field.

1. First, set up your team. Open Team and Allotment. ★ *Team & Allotment*
2. To add a Project Lead, enter their name and Encalm email address. ★ *Create Project Lead*
3. Give them a temporary password privately, never in a group chat. They choose their own when they first sign in. ★ *Temporary password*

### Step 4 · Allotting projects (~0:40)
**[SCREEN]** Same page: change a project's lead in the "Allot to" list; then pop out Reset password.

1. Next, give each Lead their projects. Choose the Lead in the Allot to list, and they can work on it straight away. ★ *Allot to*
2. If a Lead ever forgets their password, click Reset password next to their name. ★ *Reset password*

### Step 5 · Watching the portfolio (~0:50)
**[SCREEN]** Portfolio overview: key figures, health, Management attention, then the project bars.

1. With the team in place, the Portfolio overview shows how every project is doing. ★ *Key figures*
2. Here you see how many projects are on track, at risk or delayed. ★ *Portfolio health*
3. Management attention lists the projects that need help first. ★ *Needs attention*
4. Each project's bar is coloured by its schedule: green on plan, amber falling behind, red well behind or overdue. ★ *Progress bars*

### Step 6 · Approving milestones (~0:45)
**[SCREEN]** Delhi T3 International Lounge → Milestones. Pop out the pending approval; click Approve.

1. When a Lead asks for sign-off on a milestone, it waits for you. Only Coordinators can approve it. ★ *Approval · Pending*
2. Open the milestone and click Approve or Reject. The Lead sees the decision straight away. ★ *Approve / Reject*

### Step 7 · Sending an update to management (~0:50)
**[SCREEN]** Project → Email Update. Type "@Ani" and pick Anita Desai from the list, type a commentary, and pop out the Send button (not sent).

1. Finally, to send management a summary of a project, open the project and click Email Update. ★ *Email Update*
2. Type the at sign and pick each recipient from the list. ★ *Recipients*
3. Write your commentary and click Send Branded Update Email. It goes from your own Encalm mailbox. ★ *Send*

# PART 2 – PROJECT LEAD

**[ROLE TITLE CARD, ~0:25]**
> **Part 2 · Project Lead**
> **Rahul Mehta** · keeps his projects up to date
>
> **What a Project Lead can do**
> - Create new projects
> - Update the projects allotted to them: stages, milestones, issues, updates, photos and budget
> - Tag colleagues and email them from the project
> - Send Email Updates for their projects
> - View every other project (read only)

**VO:**
1. A Project Lead keeps their own projects up to date.
2. Leads can create projects and update everything on the projects allotted to them, from stages and milestones to issues, photos and budget. Other projects are view only.

**[Badge for the whole part]** PROJECT LEAD VIEW · Rahul Mehta

### Step 1 · Your first sign-in (~0:45)
**[SCREEN]** Rahul signs in with his temporary password; the Set a new password screen appears; he fills in the three fields.

1. Your Coordinator gives you a temporary password. Sign in with it, and the app asks you to choose your own. ★ *Set a new password*
2. Enter the temporary password, then a new one of at least ten characters with letters and numbers, and type it again.
3. Click Update password and continue. If you ever forget it, ask your Project Coordinator to reset it.

### Step 2 · Your dashboard (~0:40)
**[SCREEN]** The dashboard shows Rahul's projects; type "lounge" in the search box.

1. Your dashboard shows the projects allotted to you first. ★ *Your projects*
2. To find any project, type part of its name, code or location in the search box. ★ *Search*

### Step 3 · Creating the project (~1:10)
**[SCREEN]** New project. Name "Delhi T2 Departure Lounge", Location Delhi (pop out the "Other location…" option without choosing it), category, target date, AOP. Click Create project, then open it.

1. Now let's follow one project from start to finish. Click New project in the menu. ★ *New project*
2. Give it a clear name: Delhi T2 Departure Lounge. ★ *Project Name*
3. Choose the location. If it isn't on the list, choose Other location and type it in. ★ *Location · Other location…*
4. Choose the category, the target completion date and the approved budget, or AOP, in crores. Fields with a star are required. ★ *AOP*
5. Click Create project. It is allotted to you automatically, and it starts with five stages, from Brief and scope to Handover.

### Step 4 · Overview tab (~0:50)
**[SCREEN]** Delhi T2 Departure Lounge opens on Overview. Pop out status and health, Edit project, the key figures, then the tab bar, then the Overview content (project information and health).

1. The project opens on the Overview tab. We'll now go through the tabs from left to right. ★ *Tabs*
2. At the top are the project's status and health, which you keep up to date. ★ *Status · Health*
3. Click Edit project to change its name, dates, location or budget. ★ *Edit project*
4. The key figures show completion, budget and days to the target date. ★ *Key figures*
5. The Overview tab sums up the project: its details and its current health. ★ *Project information*

### Step 5 · Progress tab (~0:40)
**[SCREEN]** Progress tab: the stage table (weights, progress, contribution) and Total Project Delivery at 0%.

1. The Progress tab shows how each stage adds up to the project's total completion. ★ *Stage table*
2. A new project starts at zero. As you update stages on the next tab, Timeline, this total rises by itself. ★ *Total Project Delivery*

### Step 6 · Timeline tab: planning the stages (~1:00)
**[SCREEN]** Timeline tab. Update stage on Brief & scope: set Planned start and Planned finish, save; do the same quickly for Design development. Then pop out the colour legend over the bars.

1. On the Timeline tab, plan each stage first. Click Update stage on Brief and scope. ★ *Update stage*
2. Enter its planned start and planned finish dates, and save. Do the same for each stage. ★ *Planned start · Planned finish*
3. With dates in place, each bar's colour shows whether the stage is on schedule. ★ *Stage bars*
4. Green means ahead of schedule, yellow means work in progress on track, and red means delayed, overdue or blocked. ★ *Legend*

### Step 7 · Timeline tab: updating stages as work happens (~1:30)
**[SCREEN]** Brief & scope: Progress 100 → Status Complete (pop-out), save. Design development: type Progress 0 → 40, Status changes to In progress (pop-out); fill in Work completed and Next action; save.

1. As work happens, update each stage in order. Brief and scope is finished, so set its progress to one hundred percent. ★ *Progress %*
2. The status changes to Complete by itself. Save the stage. ★ *Status: Complete*
3. Next, Design development has started. As you type forty percent, the status changes from Not started to In progress. ★ *Status: In progress*
4. If work ever stops, set the status to Blocked yourself.
5. Write what has been done and what happens next, then click Save stage update. ★ *Work completed · Next action*
6. Your Coordinators and HODs are notified every time a stage moves.

### Step 8 · Milestones tab (~0:50)
**[SCREEN]** Milestones tab: add "Design freeze" with a date, stage and owner, tick Requires approval, Add milestone.

1. The Milestones tab holds the project's key dates. Enter a name, date, stage and owner, and click Add milestone. ★ *Add milestone*
2. If it needs sign-off, tick Requires approval, and your Coordinator is asked to approve it, as we saw in Part 1. ★ *Requires approval*
3. When a milestone is done, click Complete. ★ *Complete*

### Step 9 · Site Photograph tab (~0:40)
**[SCREEN]** Site Photograph: upload 3 photos with batch details.

1. Keep a photo record of the work on the Site photograph tab. Upload up to twenty photos at a time. ★ *Upload*
2. Set the stage, category and date once for the whole batch, and click Upload. ★ *Batch details*

### Step 10 · Commercial tab (~0:40)
**[SCREEN]** Commercial: Edit commercial data, fill in Committed and Spent till date, Save.

1. The Commercial tab tracks the money against the approved budget. ★ *Commercial*
2. Enter the committed amount and the amount spent so far, and update them regularly. ★ *Committed · Spent till date*

### Step 11 · Issues and risks tab (~0:50)
**[SCREEN]** Issues & risks: Add issue or risk "Stone delivery at risk", choose severity, Add issue; move its status to Action in progress.

1. If something threatens time, cost or quality, record it on the Issues and risks tab. ★ *Add issue or risk*
2. Choose the severity carefully. A High issue alerts every Coordinator and HOD by email. ★ *Severity*
3. As work continues, move the issue along, from Open to Closed. ★ *Status*

### Step 12 · Updates tab and tagging people (~1:10)
**[SCREEN]** Updates: Add stage update; type "@Ani", pop out the suggestion list, click Anita Desai; Post. Then back on the Timeline tab, click Tag Member on Design development, pop out the member list, tick Anita, write a message, and pop out "Will email".

1. The last tab, Updates, is the project diary. Click Add stage update, choose the stage and kind, and write what changed. ★ *Add stage update*
2. To bring someone in, type the at sign and the start of their name, then pick them from the list. ★ *Pick a person*
3. You can also tag people on any stage with Tag Member. Tick the people you need. ★ *Select members*
4. Check the Will email list, add a message, and click Tag and Send Mail. Replies come straight back to you. ★ *Will email*

## Closing (~0:25)
**[SCREEN]** The Portfolio overview, then the end card.

1. That's it. Coordinators set up the team and oversee the portfolio, and Leads take each project from start to finish, so everyone can see where every project stands.
2. If you need help, contact your Project Coordinator. Thank you for watching.
